## Correct capability descriptions

- Realtime captures speech activity/transcription and produces dialogue text. The actors' finished speech is generated separately by Higgs TTS and animated by Higgs Avatar. CUT! is not currently using Realtime speech output as the actor performance.
- The wide group listening view is a labeled illustration facing the participant, not continuous animated head turns.
- Idle clips contain subtle generated motion. Exact nods, gaze, body actions and consistent blinking across every clip are not controlled features.
- One next response can prepare while the current character speaks. The first response still needs generation; residual gaps are possible. No fixed or measured end-to-end latency is advertised.
- The delivery disclosure describes supplied TTS controls, not guaranteed emotions or specific facial poses. [Higgs TTS inline controls](https://docs.boson.ai/models/higgs-tts/overview).
